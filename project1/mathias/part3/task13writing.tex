\subsection*{Task 13}

We would like to recover the rate matrix $Q$ from the coarse observations alone.
This is an inverse problem: $Q$ generated the data in Task~12, but in practice it
is unknown, and we ask whether it can be recovered from observations taken only
every 48 months. Given the full trajectories, the
rates would follow from the unbiased estimator $q_{ij} = N_{ij}/S_i$
(equation~(2)), where $N_{ij}$ is the total number of jumps from state $i$ to $j$
and $S_i$ the total sojourn time in state $i$. These two quantities are exactly
what we would compute from the full trajectories, but the coarse screenings reveal
neither the jumps nor the time spent in each state, so they are unobserved. We
therefore treat them as latent data and recover $Q$ with the Monte-Carlo EM
algorithm given in the project, which iterates the following from an initial guess
$Q^{(0)}$ (a small rate on every transition the model permits):

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Reconstructing the hidden history (steps~1--2).} For each pair of
          consecutive observations 48 months apart, $y \to y'$, we simulate a
          complete CTMC trajectory over the interval under the current $Q^{(k)}$
          and keep it only if it ends in the observed state $y'$, resampling
          otherwise (the rejection scheme suggested in the hint). The retained
          paths are exactly the trajectories that match both the start and end
          observation, so their jumps and sojourn times are plausible
          reconstructions of what happened between screenings; counting them over
          all 1000 women gives $N_{ij}^{(k)}$ and $S_i^{(k)}$.
    \item \textbf{Updating the rates (step~3).} Once we have the reconstructed
          counts and times, we plug them into $q_{ij} = N_{ij}^{(k)}/S_i^{(k)}$
          (equation~(2)) and set each diagonal entry to the negative sum of its
          off-diagonal row (equation~(1)), giving the updated rate matrix
          $Q^{(k+1)}$.
\end{enumerate}

As suggested in the project, we monitored the change between successive iterates,
$\|Q^{(k)} - Q^{(k+1)}\|_\infty$, against the threshold $10^{-3}$.
